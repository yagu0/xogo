Same rules as draughts: pieces move diagonally by one square, and capture by jumping, potentially several units.

A capture doesn't remove anything from the board: you stack captured pieces below your own units standing on top of the pile.

The board is initially empty: in early stage, a move generally consists of placing a new unit on the board (each player got 12).

Christian Freeling and Ed van Zon (1986).
